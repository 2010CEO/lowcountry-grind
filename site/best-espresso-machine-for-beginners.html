We make espresso drinks at our coffeehouse every day, and here's the honest truth: home espresso is a hobby. It's very rewarding, but it takes a few weeks of wasted shots to get right, and the grinder matters as much as the machine.

These picks are the ones most likely to make you happy instead of frustrated. If you don't want a hobby, the moka pot at the bottom makes strong, espresso-style coffee with zero learning curve.

Questions people ask

Do I need a separate grinder?

For the Bambino Plus and Stilosa, yes, one that can grind fine for espresso. The Barista Express has one built in.

Is a moka pot espresso?

No. It brews at much lower pressure. It's strong and great for 'Cuban-style' or milk drinks, but it's its own thing.

How long until I'm good at it?

Plan on a couple of weeks and a couple of bags of beans to dial in. That's normal.
