Questions people ask

Do I need a separate grinder?

For the Bambino Plus and Stilosa, yes, one that can grind fine for espresso. The Barista Express has one built in.

Is a moka pot espresso?

No. It brews at much lower pressure. It's strong and great for 'Cuban-style' or milk drinks, but it's its own thing.

How long until I'm good at it?

Plan on a couple of weeks and a couple of bags of beans to dial in. That's normal.
