When it's 95° with 90% humidity, nobody wants hot coffee. Make a week of cold brew for less than two café drinks.

Down here, iced coffee season runs from about March to November. Cold brew is the easiest way to keep up: coarse grounds, cold water, 12 to 18 hours in the fridge, strain. Done.

Any jar can make cold brew. What a dedicated maker buys you is easy straining, less mess, and a pitcher that fits in your fridge.

Questions people ask

How long does cold brew last?

Most people keep it 1 to 2 weeks in the fridge; it tastes best in the first week.

Cold brew vs iced coffee?

Iced coffee is hot coffee chilled. Cold brew never sees heat, so it's smoother and less acidic.

What beans work best?

Medium to dark roasts. Chocolatey, nutty coffees shine as cold brew.
