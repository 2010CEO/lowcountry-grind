Questions people ask

Percolator or AeroPress?

Percolator for a crowd around a fire; AeroPress for one or two great cups with minimal weight.

Can I use pre-ground coffee?

Yes. Fresh-ground is better, but pre-ground in a sealed bag is fine for a weekend.

What about cold mornings?

Preheat your mug with hot water first. Coffee loses heat fast in a cold stand.
