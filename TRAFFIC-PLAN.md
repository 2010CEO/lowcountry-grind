# Traffic plan (the site earns $0 without clicks)

**Goal:** the 3 qualifying sales inside 180 days, then steady holiday traffic. It's Nov 20, so the **gift guide is the lead page through Dec 20**. The holidays are the best buying window of the year.

**Rule:** posts on social can link to Amazon or the site (with #ad / disclosure). **Anything printed, emailed, or texted must point to the site, never straight to Amazon** (Associates rule).

## Week 1 (setup, about 2 hours)
1. **Pinterest business account** (free; TJ creates it). Make 7 boards, one per guide. Pin 3 vertical images per guide (Canva, 1000x1500): "The Grinder a Coffeehouse Owner Would Buy First", etc. Pinterest drives search traffic for months; it's the best free channel for gift and kitchen content.
2. **Mad Hooter counter card + QR code** pointing to `/coffee-gift-guide.html` (the site, not Amazon): "Gift ideas for the coffee person in your life, picked by Mad Hooter."
3. **Facebook:** post the gift guide on TJ's page and Mad Hooter's page. Share to local buy/sell and community groups **only where the group allows promo posts**.
4. **Google Search Console** (free): submit `sitemap.xml` so the guides start getting indexed.

## Ongoing (about 30 minutes a day, 4 weeks)
- **One short vertical video a day** (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), 15-30s, shot behind the Mad Hooter bar. One tip, one product, "link in bio." Behind-the-counter credibility is the edge nobody else has.
- **5 fresh Pins a day.**
- **One new guide a week** from `keywords.txt` (`python3 lcg.py new-guide keywords.txt`). Write it in TJ's voice before publishing.
